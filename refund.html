2. Refund Requests

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, experienced a technical issue that prevented you from accessing a paid feature, or have another valid reason for requesting a refund, please contact our support team.

Each request will be reviewed individually, taking into account the circumstances, applicable consumer protection laws, and the payment provider's applicable procedures.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us using the address below. Please include:

  • The email address associated with your GTA Money Planner account.
  • The approximate date of payment.
  • The subscription plan purchased.
  • Your payment or transaction reference, if available.
  • A brief explanation of your request.

Do not send your full card number, password, or other sensitive payment credentials by email.

4. Subscription Cancellation

You may request cancellation according to the cancellation options and instructions provided during checkout or by the payment provider.

Cancellation generally prevents future renewals. It does not automatically mean that a payment already made will be refunded. Access to paid features may continue until the end of the applicable paid period, subject to the checkout terms.

5. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If you notice a duplicate charge or believe you were charged the wrong amount, contact us as soon as possible. We will investigate and work with the payment provider where necessary.

6. Processing and Refund Timing

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed through the original payment method or the payment provider's refund process. The time it takes for funds to appear depends on the payment provider, bank, and payment method.

7. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any refund, cancellation, or other consumer rights that cannot legally be limited or excluded under applicable law.
