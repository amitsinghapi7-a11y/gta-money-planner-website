GTA MONEY PLANNER

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

GTA Money Planner ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is handled when you use our website and browser extension.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GTA Money Planner, the following information may be processed:

  • Account information: Your email address and authentication-related information when you create an account or sign in.
  • Game planning data: Information you enter into the planner, such as balances, savings goals, income records, expenses, purchases, and payment methods.
  • Subscription information: Information needed to identify and manage your subscription and access to paid features.
  • Technical information: Limited technical information may be processed by the services used to operate the website, extension, authentication, and payments.

2. How Your Information Is Stored

GTA Money Planner stores your planner data locally in your browser using Chrome storage. If you sign in and use cloud synchronization, your planner data may also be synchronized and stored using Supabase services.

Authentication and subscription-related operations may also use our backend services. Information handled by these service providers is subject to their respective privacy and security practices.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used for purposes such as:

  • Providing the GTA Money Planner features.
  • Saving and synchronizing your game planning data.
  • Managing account authentication.
  • Checking subscription status and providing paid access.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, and improving the service.
  • Responding to support requests.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to help operate GTA Money Planner. These may include:

  • Supabase: For authentication and cloud data storage and synchronization.
  • Render: For hosting backend services.
  • Paddle: For payment processing and subscription management when payment services are enabled.
  • GitHub Pages: For hosting our public website.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and under their own applicable privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies.

5. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your planner data with third parties for their own advertising purposes. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the product, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information may remain stored for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Planner data stored locally may remain in your browser until you remove it or clear the relevant browser data. Cloud-stored data may require a deletion request.

To request access to or deletion of your account information or cloud-stored planner data, contact us at gtamoneyplanner.support@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We use services and technical measures intended to help protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

GTA Money Planner is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

9. Your Choices

You can choose what planner information you enter into the extension. You may also contact us to ask questions about your information or request deletion of information associated with your account.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
