Frequently Asked Questions

A few things you may want to know.

What is GTA Money Planner?

It is a money planning tool designed to help GTA players organize their in-game balances, expenses, purchases and savings goals.

Which games are supported?

GTA Money Planner currently provides separate planning areas for GTA 5 and GTA 6.

Is this an official Rockstar product?

No. GTA Money Planner is an independent fan-made planning tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Subscription management and cancellation details will be provided through the applicable checkout and account process.
